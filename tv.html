Load-Bearing Capacity


The load-bearing capacity test evaluates the strength and durability of the EcoTote paper bag by determining how much weight it can carry before showing signs of damage. Standardized weights are gradually added to assess its ability to support different loads and determine its maximum carrying capacity.

Water Resistance


The water resistance test evaluates the ability of the beeswax-coated EcoTote paper bag to withstand water exposure. Water is applied to the material to observe changes in its surface, texture, and structure, determining how effective the beeswax coating is in providing protection against moisture.

Degradability


The degradability test examines the ability of the EcoTote material to break down over time under selected environmental conditions. Through regular observation and documentation, changes in the material’s physical appearance and structure are recorded to evaluate its potential to decompose naturally.
