    What goes into one EcoTote

    Three everyday materials, each with one job. Nothing here is plastic.

    Recycled paper

    Reclaimed folders form the body of the bag and keep paper out of the bin.

    Cotton fibers

    Added to reinforce the paper so the bag can carry more weight.

    Beeswax coating

    Applied to the surface to improve resistance to water.
